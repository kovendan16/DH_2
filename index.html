What services does DH Construction offer?
We offer complete construction, renovation and interior solutions — false ceilings, glass partitions, vinyl flooring, painting, modular kitchens, wardrobes and full home/office makeovers.
Do you provide free quotes and site visits?
Yes! Site visits, measurements and detailed itemised quotes are completely free within our service areas in and around Chennai.
Which areas do you serve?
We serve Pallavaram, Tambaram, Chengalpattu, Velachery, OMR, Medavakkam and surrounding areas across Chennai.
How long does a typical renovation take?
A standard 2BHK renovation takes 30–45 days, while single services like false ceiling or flooring take 5–10 days. We share a clear timeline before starting.
Do you offer warranty on your work?
Yes, we provide workmanship warranty on all projects along with manufacturer warranties on branded materials used.
